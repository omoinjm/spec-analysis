  • 🧺
    Cache is the counter next to them — bigger means more within reach.

Reading the name

i7-13700H
Tier
i3 · i5 · i7 · i9 — like classes on a plane.
Generation
Often the most important number in the name.
Rank
Position within the tier & generation. Bigger = stronger.
Letter
Where the secret is: U saves battery · H high performance · HX monster · K overclockable desktop · F no built-in graphics.

Same idea everywhere

Intel Core Ultra 5/7/9 · AMD Ryzen 5/7/9 · Apple M chips with Pro and Max.

How to pick

  1. Check the generation.
  2. Then the tier.
  3. Then the last letter for what you need.
  4. Still unsure? Search both names with benchmark. Gaming → single-core score. Video editing → multi-core score.

⚠️ The same chip in a very thin laptop can be slower — cooling makes all the difference.
